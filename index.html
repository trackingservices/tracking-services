Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Choose a package and an enabled payment method, send the exact amount, then submit the number or account you sent from.

How long does verification take?

Payment verification takes 1-10 minutes, usually within seconds. It may take longer if the admin is offline.

Where are my tracking numbers?

After payment is verified, the order shows Paid while the admin prepares your numbers. When they are delivered, you will get a notification and can copy them from My Trackings.
